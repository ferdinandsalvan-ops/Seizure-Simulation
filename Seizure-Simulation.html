First-Aid Knowledge Guide

Core clinical guidelines for nursing students

✅ What to DO

  • • Keep the area safe from hard or sharp objects.
  • • Cushion the person's head with something soft.
  • • Turn them gently onto their side (recovery position) to keep airway clear.
  • • Time the seizure duration accurately from start to finish.
  • • Stay calm and reassure them as consciousness returns.

❌ What NOT to Do

  • • NEVER put anything in the person's mouth (risk of choking/broken teeth).
  • • Do NOT try to hold down or restrain their movements.
  • • Do NOT give water, pills, or food until fully alert and oriented.
  • • Do NOT leave the individual unattended during or immediately after.
